Paris, 1925. Con man Victor Lustig reads that the Eiffel Tower is rusting and costs a fortune to maintain. So he forges government papers, gathers a group of scrap dealers at a luxury hotel and "sells" them the tower for scrap. André Poisson takes the bait, pays up and even bribes him. Lustig leaves the country… and Poisson is too embarrassed to go to the police. Months later Lustig comes back and tries it again. This time the dealer calls the cops, and Lustig escapes. Again.
