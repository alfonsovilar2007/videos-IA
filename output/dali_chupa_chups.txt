DESCRIPTION:
Barcelona, 1958. Candy maker Enric Bernat puts a sweet on a stick and calls it "Gol"… and nobody buys it. He renames it Chupa Chups, from the Spanish verb "chupar" (to suck), and sales take off. In 1969 he asks his friend Salvador Dalí for a new wrapper. Over coffee, Dalí sketches the famous daisy with the name inside, reportedly in about an hour, and tells him to put it on top of the lollipop so it can be seen from any angle. Over 50 years later, it's still there.
