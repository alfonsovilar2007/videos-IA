1932, Western Australia. Around 20,000 emus are destroying the farmers' wheat, so the army sends Major Meredith with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds. The emus split into small groups, one gun jams, and they even outrun the truck. In the end: almost 10,000 bullets for fewer than 1,000 emus. Meredith said the birds could face machine guns "with the invulnerability of tanks."
